# SparkLeads — Complete Product Breakdown

> A customer-facing guide to what SparkLeads is, what it does, how it works, and what makes it unique.

---

## 1. What SparkLeads Is

**SparkLeads is an all-in-one client-finding and client-closing machine for freelancers, agencies, and consultants.**

The core promise: *"Find 200+ real, qualified business leads in 60 seconds."* You type any business type in any city in the world — "Plumbers in Lagos," "Dentists in Dubai," "Gyms in London" — and SparkLeads streams in real businesses with their actual phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, and Google ratings.

But it doesn't stop at finding leads. SparkLeads walks you through the **entire sales journey** — from finding the business, to auditing their website and Google profile (so you know exactly what to pitch), to generating a professional proposal, to writing personalized outreach messages, to sending emails and WhatsApp messages, to tracking follow-ups until you close the deal. It's a lead list, sales pitch generator, and outreach tool in one.

**Who it's built for:** web designers, social media marketing agencies (SMMAs), SEO agencies, copywriters, sales teams, WhatsApp marketers, consultants, and virtual assistants — anyone who sells services to local businesses.

## 2. Complete Feature List

### Pillar 1 — Find Leads
- **Global lead search** — search any business type in any city across 195+ countries
- **Live streaming results** — leads appear one by one on screen as they're found (like a live feed), so you see value within seconds
- **Full contact details** — real phone numbers, emails, addresses, websites, and Google star ratings per business
- **Email discovery engine** — automatically finds business email addresses by scanning their websites
- **One-click CSV export** — download your entire list and import it into any CRM or spreadsheet
- **Smart search suggestions** — auto-suggests neighborhoods and areas (e.g., searching "restaurants in Lagos" suggests Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikeja) to help you dig deeper into specific districts
- **Search history** — every search is saved and can be reopened

### Pillar 2 — Manage Your Pipeline
- **Lead status tracking** — every lead moves through a pipeline: New → Contacted → Interested → Closed → Not Interested
- **My Leads** — a central database of every business you've saved, filterable by status, notes, and opportunity score
- **Opportunity scoring** — AI scores each lead on how good a fit it is for *your* specific service
- **Private notes** — attach notes to any lead
- **Follow-up reminders** — schedule reminders so no deal ever goes cold (with due-date notifications)

### Pillar 3 — Audit & Pitch (the "consultant in a box")
- **Website Grader** — paste any website URL and get an instant score out of 100 covering speed, mobile-friendliness, SEO, security, and user experience, plus a prioritized list of fixes
- **Google Business Profile Auditor** — analyzes a business's Google profile: categories, reviews, photos, posts, and Q&A, scored out of 100
- **Full Digital Audit Report** — combines the website + Google profile into one comprehensive, unified report with benchmarks and an action plan — the perfect sales weapon for showing a business owner everything they're leaving on the table
- **Competitor Analysis** — pulls a business's competitors from Google Maps and compares ratings, reviews, photos, and online presence side-by-side

### Pillar 4 — Close the Deal
- **AI Proposals** — generates a complete, professional proposal in minutes: your services, pricing tiers, timelines, and ROI projections, branded with your agency name, printable as a document and shareable via WhatsApp
- **AI Messages** — writes personalized WhatsApp, SMS, email, and call scripts tailored to each business type and pain point, with a personalization hook for every lead
- **Bulk WhatsApp + Bulk Email outreach** — select multiple leads and send messages in bulk directly from the platform, with per-message delivery results
- **Message templates** — save your best-performing scripts and reuse them

### Pillar 5 — Keep Clients (and Find More)
- **AI Content Generator** — produce a month of social media posts with captions, hashtags, and optimal posting times
- **Content Calendar** — see a month-at-a-glance calendar of scheduled posts
- **Content Library** — store and reuse everything you've generated
- **Ad Campaign Planner** — generates Google Ads and Facebook ad copy with keyword targeting, budget allocation, and audience segments
- **Creative Briefs** — produces detailed brand briefs covering brand voice, color palette, typography, target audience, content pillars, video scripts, and image directions

### Pillar 6 — Grow Into an Agency
- **Agency workspace** — a full team environment with a branded workspace (your logo and brand color)
- **Client management board** — track clients across Prospect / Active / Paused / Churned, with monthly retainer amounts
- **Team seats & roles** — Owner, Manager, and Member roles with controlled access
- **Invite links** — generate shareable team invites with credit limits per member
- **Credit allocation** — distribute your monthly credits to team members
- **Agency analytics** — business metrics, credit health, and team performance dashboards
- **Activity feed** — see what your team is doing across the workspace
- **Billing & usage tracking** — plan management and monthly usage overview

### Pillar 7 — Monetization & Extras
- **Affiliate program** — 50% commission ($7.50 per sale), personal referral link, payout requests with bank details, and ready-made WhatsApp/Twitter promo templates
- **Credits system** — pay-as-you-go credits for individual users, with live balance shown in the sidebar
- **Full legal & trust package** — terms, privacy, refund (7-day money-back), acceptable use, and cookie policies

## 3. The Exact User Journey (from Signup to Closed Client)

### Step 1 — First click
A visitor lands on the homepage and sees: how it works (3 simple steps), 12 feature highlights, testimonials, live "someone just activated SparkLeads" notifications, and a clear **"Sign Up Free"** button. The pitch is instant: 200+ leads, 60 seconds, one-time price, no monthly fee.

### Step 2 — Free trial (zero risk)
They click through to the **free trial page**, create an account with just an email and password, and get **3 free lead searches — no credit card required**.

### Step 3 — The "wow" moment (core value in 60 seconds)
They immediately land on the **search dashboard**, type "Plumbers in Lagos," and watch real businesses **stream in live** — names, phones, emails, ratings, addresses appearing one by one. They can copy numbers, save leads, and even export to CSV. This is where the product sells itself.

### Step 4 — The paywall → purchase
After the 3 free searches, a paywall appears. They're offered **lifetime access for a one-time payment of ₦19,900 (~$15)** — unlimited searches forever. Payment is handled securely through Paystack (card, bank transfer, etc.).

### Step 5 — Activation
After paying, they receive an **activation link by email**. One click on the link unlocks the full account ("Your SparkLeads account is ready!") and they're back in the dashboard with full access.

### Step 6 — Choose your path
A quick onboarding screen asks: **Individual or Agency?**
- **Individual** → straight into the dashboard with pay-as-you-go credits
- **Agency** → a 3-step setup: name your workspace and pick a brand color → choose a plan (Starter $49 / Growth $99 / Pro $199 per month) → generate team invite links

### Step 7 — The daily workflow (find → audit → pitch → close)
1. **Search** for leads in any niche + city
2. **Save leads**, jot notes, mark status, set follow-up reminders
3. **Grade their website** and **audit their Google profile** to find their weaknesses
4. **Generate a proposal** tailored to them and send it via WhatsApp or print it
5. **Write personalized outreach messages** (AI-written, with a personalization hook)
6. **Send bulk WhatsApp or emails** right from the platform
7. **Track everything** in the pipeline until the client is closed
8. **Keep them** with monthly content, ad plans, and calendars — all generated in minutes

### Step 8 — Grow and earn
Users can invite team members (agency), monitor analytics, and join the **affiliate program** to earn $7.50 per sale by sharing their referral link.

## 5. What Makes It Genuinely Unique

**1. Results appear live, not after a wait.**
Most lead tools make you wait for a spinner. SparkLeads **streams results in real time** — businesses appear one by one as they're found. Users see dozens of real leads within the first minute. This "wow in 60 seconds" moment is the entire conversion strategy: try it free, watch it work, then pay.

**2. It's a full sales pipeline, not just a list.**
Competitors sell a lead list and walk away. SparkLeads covers the entire funnel: find leads → audit their website and Google profile (the perfect cold-call hook) → auto-generate a proposal → write personalized outreach → send it → track follow-ups → manage the client. One subscription replaces five tools.

**3. Built for emerging markets and WhatsApp-first outreach.**
Payments via Paystack (Nigeria/Africa standard), prices in naira (₦19,900), WhatsApp integration baked in (composers, bulk sending, shareable proposals and messages), and phone numbers formatted for WhatsApp. It's designed for the way SMMA owners and freelancers in Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, and Dubai actually sell — over WhatsApp.

**4. One account, two products.**
The same login serves a **solo freelancer** (lifetime one-time fee + pay-as-you-go credits) and a **multi-person agency** (monthly plans with team seats, client boards, credit allocation, and role-based access). You start as an individual and upgrade to an agency workspace without a new account.

**5. Pricing model is intentionally aggressive.**
A **one-time ₦19,900 (~$15) lifetime purchase** — not a subscription — with unlimited searches, backed by a 7-day money-back guarantee and an affiliate program paying 50% commission per sale. That's an unusual, high-trust offer designed for viral growth via affiliates.

**6. Smart sales tools you don't see elsewhere.**
- **Opportunity scoring**: AI tells you which leads are the best fit for *your* service (freelancer profile based)
- **Pipeline progress tracking**: each lead shows whether it's been graded, audited, proposed to, and contacted — so you never lose track of where a deal stands
- **Website scraping for emails**: finds business emails other tools can't
- **Neighborhood expansion**: one city search suggests district-level searches to multiply your lead count
- **Client board**: agencies run their whole book of business inside the same tool

**7. Trust and safety built in.**
Free trial with no card required, secure activation links, server-side payment verification, rate limiting, and complete legal pages — the kind of polish that matters when real money is involved.
